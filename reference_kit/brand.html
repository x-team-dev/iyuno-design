Brand

Logo, mark, voice, and the rules around all of them.

A "refresh, not rebrand." The wordmark stays. We made amber load-bearing, replaced cool grays with warm ink, and unified the system on one brand sans — Switzer — that matches the wordmark's DNA.

Wordmark

Do & don't

COLOR PAIRINGS · WORDMARK USAGE
DO · ink + paper + amber accent

Wordmark in white on ink. The dot stays amber. Reserved, confident, our default treatment for keynotes and footers.

DON'T · cool gradient backgrounds

No purple-to-cyan AI gradients. They contradict the warm system and read as generic AI startup chrome.

DO · amber surface, mono amber lockup

When the surface is amber itself, switch to the dark mono lockup. The dot disappears — that's correct.

DON'T · recolor the wordmark
iyuno

Never paint the wordmark in any non-system color. Black, white, ink-950, or mono-amber only.

Voice in two columns

WHAT WE SOUND LIKE / DON'T
Posture Quiet expertise. We say less, more carefully. Hype, stacked superlatives, "revolutionary."
Subject The creator and their work. We are the infrastructure. "AI-powered," "reimagined with AI," "the future of."
Pronouns "You" addresses the studio. "We" is Iyuno, used sparingly. Plural "users," "customers" in body copy.
Sentence shape Short. Declarative. One idea per sentence. Long, comma-stitched, "leveraging cutting-edge AI to…"
Numbers "312 cues across 4 locales." Specific, verifiable. "Best-in-class accuracy." (Best at what? Class of what?)
Casing Sentence case for headlines. Mono ALL CAPS only as eyebrows. Title Case In Marketing Headlines.

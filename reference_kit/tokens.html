How to use

Every token is a CSS variable in colors_and_type.css. Reference with var(--name).

01 · Color

Warm amber, warm ink. No cool grays.

Two scales do almost everything: amber (signature, hue 75–85) for action and emphasis, ink (warm neutrals, hue 80) for surface and text. Semantic colors are warm-leaning — even our blues and reds tilt slightly into amber.

02 · Type

One sans for voice. One sans for clarity. Mono for the machine.

One family, three jobs. Inter handles every voice from 144px hero to 11px caption — hierarchy comes from size, weight, and tracking. Mono is reserved for timecodes, IDs, and metadata. Don't reach for a serif; the wordmark is sans, the system is sans. Company.

03 · Type scale

Ten steps. Use sparingly.

Most pages need 3–4 sizes total. Mixing more produces noise. Default to --fs-base (15px) for body and step up for titles.

04 · Spacing

4px base. Generous breathing room.

Most components live in the 16–32px range. Use --sp-12 (48px) and up for section breaks. We err on the side of more whitespace, not less.

05 · Radii

10px is the signature.

Derived from the proportion of the dot above the i in our wordmark. --r-md (10px) is the default for buttons, cards, inputs, and panels. --r-full (capsules) only for status dots and avatar shapes.

06 · Shadow

Soft, warm-tinted, never blue.

Shadows are warm-ink, low opacity, layered. Use --shadow-xs on resting cards, --shadow-md on hover. Anything heavier is for modals.

07 · Motion

Restrained, intentional, scroll-triggered.

One easing curve does almost all the work. Three durations cover the rest. No autoplay. No loops. Motion narrates user action, never demands attention.

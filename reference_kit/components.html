Button

--accent · --r-md · --d-fast · --shadow-glow

Primary CTAs use amber. Secondary buttons sit on a hairline border. Ghost for inline tertiary actions. Never put two primary buttons side by side — pick one hero action.

Input

--border-strong · --r-md · --shadow-glow on focus

Single-line text inputs. Focus ring is amber, not blue. Help text uses mono to mark it as system metadata.

Card

--surface · --r-md · --shadow-xs · --border

A surface for grouped content. White background, hairline border, shadow-xs at rest, shadow-sm on hover. Eyebrow + display title is the canonical pattern.

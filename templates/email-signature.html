Gmail

  • Settings ⚙ → See all settings → General → Signature.
  • Click "Create new", paste the rendered preview (not the code).
  • Set "For new emails use" and "On reply/forward" to your two signatures.

Outlook (desktop & web)

  • File → Options → Mail → Signatures (desktop), or Settings → Mail → Compose & reply (web).
  • Paste the rendered preview. Outlook will keep table structure intact.
  • Logo image is hosted at x-team-dev.github.io/iyuno-design/assets/iyuno-logo.svg — never inline.

Apple Mail

  • Mail → Settings → Signatures → drag the rendered preview into the right pane.
  • Uncheck "Always match my default message font" so styling sticks.
  • Test once: send to yourself before adopting.

Brand rules for signatures

  • Always use the hosted PNG logo at 20px tall — never inline SVG, it breaks in Outlook.
  • Always use Arial/Helvetica as the primary font and Courier New as the mono fallback. Inter and JetBrains Mono are not safe in email.
  • Always use table-based layout. Never use flex, grid, or CSS variables — they will silently fail.
  • Never add quotes, taglines, or pronouns inside the signature unless your role explicitly calls for them.
  • Never stack social icons. We do not have a corporate social signature.
  • Never use the campaign banner block for more than 6 weeks at a time, and only for an active company-wide initiative.

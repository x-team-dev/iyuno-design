What changes

Wider contribution

AI makes many kinds of work easier to start.

A marketer can build a website
A product manager can create a working demo
A language expert can design a quality check
An operations team can build a simple tool
People can contribute beyond the traditional boundaries of their roles.

The interface becomes easier. The business problem does not become simpler.

AI expands capability; it does not remove expertise.03 / 17
The new division of work

From doing every step to guiding the result

AI can help with the work. People must own the outcome.

More of the work AI can do

  • Create a first draft
  • Search and organize information
  • Repeat known steps
  • Compare many options
  • Find common errors

More of the work people must own

  • Set the goal
  • Explain the situation
  • Define what “good” means
  • Make difficult trade-offs
  • Take responsibility for the result
Our interpretation: execution becomes easier; purpose and judgment become more valuable.04 / 17
